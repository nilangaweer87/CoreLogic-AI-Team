# Touch POS and ticket workflow

1. Walk through the actual shift using touch input: sign in, select table, add food and drink, save order, settle, print bill and close shift. Check focus and touch targets on the smallest supported screen.

2. Route new or amended lines to the right kitchen or bar printer. Record dispatch and acknowledgment separately. Make a deliberate reprint recognizable when printer confirmation is uncertain.

3. Test double taps, lost Wi-Fi after Save, repeated settlement and split tender. Preserve the transaction reference across retries. Make pending, succeeded and actionable failure states legible.

4. Exercise manager approval for served and unserved cancellations. Verify the receipt, ticket history, reason and stock consequences against the agreed policy.
